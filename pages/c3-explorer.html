What you're practising

Horizontal and vertical motion are independent. Predict the range first, using R = u² sin 2θ / g, then launch. Try 30° and 60°: why do they land in the same place? Reader §3.1.

Challenge

Find two different angles that give the same range. Then find the angle with the longest range.

What you're practising

Series: one current through both, voltages add, resistances add. Parallel: full voltage across each, currents add, combined resistance is smaller than either. Power P = VI decides which bulb would glow brighter. Reader §3.6.

What you're practising

Superposition: waves add point by point. In phase, they reinforce; 180° out of phase with equal amplitude, they cancel. Two equal waves travelling in opposite directions make a standing wave with fixed nodes. Slightly different frequencies make beats. Reader §3.5.

Challenge

Make (1) total cancellation, (2) a standing wave, and (3) beats. Which settings did each need?
