    What you're practising

    Predict before you calculate. Write the law as a proportionality, isolate the output, then raise the input's factor to the matching power. Decimals are fine: one quarter is 0.25.

    Why this matters

    Scaling is the fastest check on any rearrangement (Reader §11, test four), and it answers every "by what factor" exam question in seconds. It is the same reasoning as interpreting an elasticity: a one-per-cent change in input produces an n-per-cent change in output.
