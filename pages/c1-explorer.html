What you're practising

The law of large numbers: the observed fraction wanders at first, then settles towards the true probability. The shaded band is ±2 standard errors, which shrinks as 1/√n. Quadrupling the rolls only halves the band. Reader §1.5.

Challenge

Before rolling 1000 times: within what range do you expect the fraction of sixes to land about 95% of the time? Enter the half-width (for example 0.05).

What you're practising

Vectors add tip to tail, and the components simply add. The dot product a·b = |a||b|cos θ is zero when the vectors are perpendicular and negative when they point more than 90° apart. Reader §1.6.
