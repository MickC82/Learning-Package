What you're practising

Protons decide the element; neutrons change the mass and make isotopes; electrons decide the charge and, through the outer shell, the chemistry. Shells fill 2, 8, 8 for the first twenty elements. Reader §4.1–4.2.

What you're practising

25.0 mL of acid is titrated with 0.10 M sodium hydroxide. The pH changes slowly, then leaps at the equivalence point, where moles of base equal moles of acid. A weak acid starts higher and its equivalence point is above pH 7. Reader §4.7.
